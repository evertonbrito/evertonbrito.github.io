ASTREIA RECOVERY SCENE — ASSET PROVENANCE

Helicopter: Eurocopter EC135 P2, a member of the Airbus Helicopters family.
Original 3D geometry and textures: Heiko Schulz / FlightGear.
Source: https://github.com/HHS81/ec135
Pinned revision: 6c502e2a8772c3e796706f59f78ee626071a75d6
License: GNU GPL version 2 (see ec135-GPL-2.0.txt).
Corresponding source and conversion script: ec135-source.zip (in this directory).
Adaptations: selected medium skids and winch configuration, removed alternate
radomes and equipment variants, converted AC3D with original UVs to GLB,
painted offline rendering and eight main-rotor positions.
This is a community model, not an official Airbus CAD asset.

Recovery platform: original parametric mesh from the earlier authoring pipeline
(recoverable in Git at 5f12c36:src/authoring/RecoveryAssets.js).
Visual references: Blue Origin Jacklyn and SpaceX autonomous droneships.
https://www.blueorigin.com/gallery/asset/6931c2f7ec50597c42d6943b
https://www.spacex.com/vehicles/falcon-9/
Original Astreia markings; no claim to reproduce certified flight or marine hardware.

Production uses baked 2D WebP views. The EC135 GLB is in authoring/recovery/ec135.glb, for local authoring only.


14 October 2026 painted derivative: recovery-painted/helicopter.webp is a
gouache/ink style transfer of the existing EC135 sprite, generated with the
built-in image_gen tool; main rotor animation remains a separate 2D layer.
The original aircraft geometry source and GPL-2.0 license remain available here.
recovery-painted/barge.webp is a matching painted derivative of the existing
Astreia recovery-platform illustration. Exact generation prompts and source
images are retained in authoring/illustrations/recovery/.
